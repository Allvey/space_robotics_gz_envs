The original model.sdf, model.config, r2.dae, material scripts, and three PBR
texture images in this directory are unchanged copies from the project's
existing Fuel cache. Their hashes are recorded in source_manifest.json.
The publisher's license metadata is preserved in fuel_metadata.json.

Adaptations in sibling lunar_excavator and lunar_hauler directories:
New payload frames, mining arm, scoop, tipping cargo bed and tailgate;
relocated rear RGB-D camera, additional drive / joint controllers, updated
SDF syntax, default vehicle lamps off and hidden light visualizations.
Original chassis, wheels, center pivot, mass / inertia, and PBR atlas retained.
These are project-created conceptual mining adaptations, not official Team
Explorer or NASA mining vehicle designs. The combined Blender scene and
rendered previews incorporate the above attributed vehicle assets.
